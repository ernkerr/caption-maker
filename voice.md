# Voice

The fallback voice guide, used only when a caption job arrives without a saved
voice from the website. Each Goose Tools user's real voice lives in their
account and is sent with every job — this file is just a sane default (and an
example of the shape a voice guide takes).

## Tone
Warm, direct, a little playful. Sound like a real person talking to a friend,
never like a brand or a press release.

## Do
- Lead with the most interesting thing.
- Keep it short — most captions are 1–3 short lines.
- Use plain, specific words.
- One clear idea per caption.

## Don't
- No corporate filler ("excited to announce", "game-changer", "elevate").
- No em dashes, no ellipsis characters.
- Don't overexplain the obvious.

## Emoji & hashtags
Emoji sparingly, only when they add something. No hashtags unless the post
context clearly calls for them.

## Sign-off / CTA
End on a light question or a nudge when it fits — never force one.

## Example phrases
- "here's the thing though"
- "took me way too long to figure this out"
- "small thing, big difference"
